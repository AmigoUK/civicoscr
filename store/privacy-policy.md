# Privacy Policy — Civico.net Downloader

_Last updated: 6 August 2026_

Civico.net Downloader is a Chrome extension that downloads publicly available
audio (MP3) and video (MP4) recordings of council meeting webcasts hosted on
the Civico platform (civico.net).

## Data collection

The extension does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data or user data of any kind. It has no analytics, no tracking, no
accounts, and no remote code.

## What the extension accesses

- **The active tab's URL** (via the `activeTab` permission), only at the
  moment you click the extension icon, and only to pre-fill the popup's URL
  field when you are on a civico.net page. Page content is never read or
  modified, and the URL is not stored or transmitted anywhere except to
  civico.net servers as part of the download request you initiate.
- **civico.net servers** (`admin.civico.net`, `vod.civico.net`), contacted
  only when you click Download, to fetch the meeting's public metadata and
  media files.
- **Chrome's downloads system** (via the `downloads` permission), used to
  save the MP3/MP4 files you requested to your Downloads folder.

## Third parties

No data is sent to any third party. The only network requests the extension
makes are to civico.net's own servers, and only at your explicit request.
